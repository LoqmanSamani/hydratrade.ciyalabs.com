HydraTrade

Machine learning on markets, with the failures on record.

HydraTrade studies systematic trading in cryptocurrency and US equities. Every test is written down before it runs, each universe is rebuilt as it was known at the time, and the changes that fail are published beside the ones that hold. The reports below form one series: each starts from what the one before it established.

Method

Four rules every report follows, so a result can be checked rather than trusted.

  1. Registered firstEvery change, its parameters and the test it must pass are fixed in writing before the first run. A rule that is loosened later is reported as such.
  2. Point-in-time universesCoins and companies enter and leave the universe on the dates they actually did, delisted names included. Report II shows what happens otherwise.
  3. Walk-forward with an embargoModels learn only from the past and are tested on the window that follows, with a gap between the two so that labels cannot leak.
  4. Failures on recordA change that fails is written up in the same detail as one that holds. Most of what the series has tested failed, and that is part of the result.
